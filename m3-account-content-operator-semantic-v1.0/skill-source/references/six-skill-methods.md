# Six-Skill Method Inheritance

Load this reference when M3 creates downstream tasks, evaluates a valid equivalent input, or checks that commercial objectives did not erase applicable professional value.

The methods below are obligations to preserve when applicable. They do not require running every physical Skill, and they do not authorize M3 to produce downstream artifacts.

## 1. Matrix

Preserve:

- account responsibility before decorative personality;
- identifiable expression subject and source ownership;
- audience, follow reason, content value, and long-term promise consistency;
- the difference between confirmed fact, professional interpretation, and provisional positioning;
- return to Matrix when the long-term identity itself may be wrong.

Do not:

- infer a new long-term positioning from short-term performance;
- let two accounts become interchangeable after removing names;
- rewrite persona, account responsibility, or multi-account relations inside M3.

M3 handoff duty: include the currently valid positioning/voice reference or an explicitly provisional anchor and its limits.

## 2. Campaign

Preserve:

- a clear validity window and one primary business/customer change;
- explicit operating trade-offs and non-sacrifice conditions;
- the minimum sufficient participating combination;
- the ability to reduce, defer, cancel, or not publish;
- named coverage of the cycle and a return condition.

Do not:

- make Campaign the only legal upstream;
- narrow traffic, followers, GMV, leads, or visits into a generic cognitive-change goal;
- let a temporary Campaign permanently rewrite Matrix or the cycle baseline.

M3 handoff duty: expose the active overlay, occupied slots, unaffected baseline, and expiry.

## 3. Content Brief

Preserve:

- one primary job per content item with limited secondary contribution;
- a specific audience problem, scene, or opportunity;
- the desired audience change;
- a content promise, core proposition, or testable hypothesis;
- fact, source, asset, production, CTA, and risk boundaries;
- one highest-discrimination question when the task cannot proceed safely.

Do not:

- force every task through Matrix and Campaign;
- substitute a goal label and cadence for the content’s business substance;
- ask the user to refill information already present in natural language or valid artifacts.

M3 handoff duty: supply enough business meaning for Brief to begin, then stop.

## 4. Creative Tournament and Creative Script

Preserve:

- real room for materially different creative directions when a genuine choice exists;
- differences in mechanism, not just wording, labels, examples, or tone;
- tension as an unfinished state when relevant, not forced conflict;
- fact zones, expressive freedom, subjective limits, and explicit non-promises;
- an identifiable human expression subject and content-specific detail;
- the option to recommend one direction directly when alternatives are artificial.

Do not:

- prescribe hook, narrative structure, dialogue, or visual mechanism in M3;
- demand narrative or empathy for lists, comparisons, demonstrations, product details, or visual-mechanism content when not applicable;
- present unknown facts as a finished script premise.

M3 handoff duty: preserve the business problem and boundaries while leaving creative mechanism open.

## 5. Production Director

Preserve:

- a distinction between planned realization and actual captured material;
- production constraints early enough to affect feasibility;
- resource changes altering HOW rather than silently changing the business job;
- the requirement that production guidance create executable differences;
- proof claims having failure conditions.

Do not:

- write shots, performance direction, scenes, or realization manifests in M3;
- treat “planned to capture,” “shot for a long time,” or “asset exists” as “the content promise was realized”;
- demand full production resources when a lower-resource route can fully deliver the same job.

M3 handoff duty: state actual capacity, critical proof/material needs, and conditions that would make the task infeasible.

## 6. Publishing & Packaging

Preserve:

- publication state derived from actual available material;
- packaging promise no greater than the realized payoff;
- current platform, account, permission, CTA, inventory, price, and fulfillment truth;
- explicit non-promises as read-only downstream constraints;
- clean user delivery without rejected alternatives or internal review debris.

Do not:

- write title, cover, entry copy, or final CTA in M3;
- package planned, missing, or simulated material as realized;
- infer high-risk expression, external diversion, discount, stock, or purchase promise from a commercial objective.

M3 handoff duty: pass objective, CTA intent, authorization boundaries, current facts, and invalidation conditions.

## 7. Conditional applicability

Use these questions before preserving an obligation:

1. Does the current task type need this capability to avoid a unique failure?
2. Is the necessary business semantic already present in an equivalent input?
3. Would removing the obligation change the downstream result under the current acceptance criteria?
4. Does enforcing it create a false all-capability gate?

If the obligation does not change the result, omit it. If a valid equivalent input preserves it, do not force a physical call. If it is necessary and absent, block only the dependent branch or return one discriminating question.

## 8. Acceptance split

M3 can prove only that its decision and handoff preserve the opportunity for applicable professional value. M3 cannot alone prove the finished item has a superior hook, narrative, human feel, persona, shootability, conversion naturalness, or packaging. Test finished-content gain at integration level with controlled comparison.

Never use model self-rating, longer output, more components, or more rules as evidence of professional superiority.
